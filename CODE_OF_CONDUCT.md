# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We expect contributors to be respectful, constructive, and precise. Critique code, assumptions, data quality, and behavior rather than people. Make space for different experience levels and communicate in good faith.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private information, and disruptive personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report concerns privately through the repository owner or project maintainers rather than posting sensitive details publicly.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant principles and applies to project spaces and project-related communication.
